# Step 4.1 — Protocol clarifications (addendum to Step 3.7)

This addendum clarifies wording only. It does **not** edit `evaluation_protocol.md`, `sensitivity_analysis_report.md`, any manifest, split or role. Where it differs in emphasis from Step 3.7 prose, this addendum governs interpretation.

1. **Exposure terminology.** Exactly **17** test records have *identified cross-boundary exposure*: they have an exact-duplicate partner in original folds 0–8 (13 of them with a partner the `ft_fold0` backbone trained on). The other duplicate-involved test records (23 − 17 = 6) are one within-test pair (2 records) and 4 records whose partner lies outside the experiment set; they are *duplicate-involved* but are **not** cross-boundary contamination. Cohort B removes the 17; cohort C removes all 23 as a stricter sensitivity check. Reports must not describe all 23 as cross-boundary.

2. **Optimism from historical validation reuse is unknown.** Fold 0 informed the backbone's epoch/threshold/weak-class selection, and historical validation folds contained duplicate copies of training signals. The size of the resulting optimism has **not been measured** and must not be asserted as small or large. The small sensitivity differences of Step 3.7 are descriptive, not a bound or causal estimate.

3. **Roles and cohorts stay fixed.** Training (35,568), validation (4,437), test_reserved (4,447), the 24 in-experiment exclusions, and evaluation cohorts A (4,447), B (4,430, main), C (4,424) are unchanged by Step 4.1. No new split, no re-assignment of excluded records.

4. **Rare-class threshold fallback — to be specified before any threshold fitting.** Validation support is very thin for several classes (e.g., ≤ 3 positives for six classes and none for AVRT). Before any future classification threshold or calibration is fitted, the protocol must declare, in advance, what happens when a class lacks enough validation positives (for example: minimum-support rule, pooling or a fixed default, or reporting that class without a tuned threshold). **No fallback is selected or fitted in Step 4.1**, and nothing may be tuned on the test fold.

5. **Concept development vs assessment.** Concept extraction methods, parameters and quality/masking rules are to be developed on **training-role data only**, then frozen and assessed on **held-out data** (validation for iteration decisions, test cohort B once for final reporting) using the fixed rules and, where available, trusted reference annotations. Because the dataset contains no reference measurements, any claim about physiological accuracy requires a separately sourced reference (expert annotations or an external annotated database) and cannot be made from this dataset's labels alone. Diagnostic codes are not independent ground truth for the measurements they describe.

6. **Model-specific rules are still to be declared.** Before any training: the concept-layer and prototype-head architecture, loss and its weights, training/early-stopping criteria, prototype count and projection schedule, handling of the backbone-exposure gap (features of train-role records come from signals the backbone fitted), concept-masking behaviour, random seeds, and the exact validation-based selection procedure must be written down and frozen in a pre-declaration, in the same spirit as the Step 3.7 cohort declaration.

7. **Batch/period structure is a tracked covariate.** Step 4.1 found ID-range structure in the headers (a contiguous block of 2021-timestamped records with a different label mix; missing/zero ages only at higher IDs). ID range, timestamp presence and age-missingness must be recorded as covariates for stratified checks of any extracted concept and must never be used as model inputs.
